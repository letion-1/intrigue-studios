The problem

A brokerage does not lose deals on price. It loses them on speed.

Availability is scattered

Every operator sends a different spreadsheet, in a different format, on a different day. Nobody on the desk trusts the version they are looking at.

Inquiries are re-typed by hand

A client email becomes a manual CRM entry, then a manual availability check, then a manual proposal. Three people touch it before anything goes out.

Knowledge sits in individual inboxes

When a broker is on the water or on leave, their pipeline is invisible to the rest of the team.

What Bahari OS does

One database. Every yacht, every inquiry, every client, every proposal.

Most brokerages run five or six disconnected tools. A spreadsheet for availability, an inbox for inquiries, a shared drive for documents, a CRM half the desk ignores, and a Word template for proposals. Nothing talks to anything, so every handover is a copy and paste.

Bahari OS puts all of it on one database. A yacht exists once. A client exists once. An availability window exists once. Every screen is a different view onto the same records, which is why a status change in one place is instantly true everywhere else.

The availability engine

The part that is actually hard

Anyone can build a CRM. The reason brokerages still live in spreadsheets is that supplier availability arrives in a hundred incompatible formats.

Bahari OS reads six different spreadsheet layouts out of the box. When it meets a seventh it uses AI to infer the structure, then validates the result before anything enters your fleet. It reads the colour convention an operator actually uses rather than the one they documented, extracts split charters and embarkation ports into fields you can filter on, and only surfaces windows that are genuinely open.

Connect a Google Sheet, a Dropbox folder, an operator website or a PDF once. It re-syncs on a schedule without anyone touching it. That last part is the one no competitor can copy by looking at the interface.

Questions we actually get

The objections worth answering first

Do you publish or expose my availability anywhere?
No. Bahari OS is internal only. It publishes nothing, lists nothing publicly, and makes nothing bookable by a third party. Your availability, rates and client records are visible to your workspace and to nobody else. There is no marketplace, no shared inventory pool, and no directory that other brokerages can browse.
What if an operator sends a format you have never seen?
Six common layouts are handled directly. Anything unfamiliar goes through AI structure inference, and the result is validated before it enters your fleet rather than after. If a file genuinely cannot be read, it is flagged for a person instead of being silently half imported. In practice the second file from the same operator is already recognised, because the system remembers the shape of that source. If a source needs an API connection or password authentication to reach, we set that up for you during implementation rather than leaving it to your team.
How long does setup take?
Connecting a first source and seeing your fleet on screen takes about an afternoon. There is no migration phase, because Bahari OS reads the sources you already keep rather than asking you to move into a new database. Adoption can be gradual, and you do not have to replace everything on day one.
What happens to my data if I leave?
You can export everything in standard formats at any point, including after cancellation. There is no lock in mechanism, and no period where your records are held while a contract winds down.
Does this replace my yacht management software?
No. Maintenance, crew payroll, ISM compliance and marine accounting stay where they are. Bahari OS covers the commercial side, which is the path from fleet information to a delivered charter.
